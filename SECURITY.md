## 2. Reporting a Vulnerability

If you discover a security vulnerability within `stratum-cli` or `stratum-core`, please send an email to **contacto@sandrapuerto.com** with:
1. Description of the vulnerability.
2. Steps or script to reproduce the issue.
3. Potential impact on multi-tenant boundary isolation.

Reports will be acknowledged within 48 hours and coordinated disclosure will be managed responsibly.
